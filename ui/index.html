01

Tournament

Connect a wallet and load a tournament to see actions for your role.

Load a tournament to see its rules, deadlines and status.

Actions use the connected role. Rules are enforced by the contract. Cancellation is available to the organizer before finalization; participants then recover their deposits.

02

LRT exchange

Buy or redeem LRT at the fixed rate. Redemptions depend on available ETH reserves.

Transfers your existing LRT to the deployed exchange. The inventory can be purchased by other users.

Redemption ETH is credited before withdrawal.

Reserve contributions are permanent backing. There is no administrator withdrawal.

03

New tournament

The connected wallet becomes the organizer. The full ETH prize is escrowed when the tournament is created; the optional LRT bonus is transferred when registration opens. These are editable demo defaults. All three role addresses must differ.

04

Results & review

Text is hashed in your browser. Only its hash is stored on-chain. Keep the evidence itself available off-chain. A resolver confirms the original ranking or submits a corrected one.
